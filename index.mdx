Hookline is a webhook delivery platform. You send an event to Hookline with one API call, and Hookline delivers it to every endpoint your customers have registered. It signs each request, retries failures on a fixed schedule, and keeps a searchable log of every attempt.

Use Hookline when you want to offer webhooks in your product without building and running the delivery infrastructure yourself.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Model your customers as applications">
    Create one [application](/sending/applications) for each customer that receives webhooks from you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let customers add endpoints">
    Customers register HTTPS URLs as [endpoints](/getting-started/core-concepts), either through your UI and the API or through the embeddable [consumer portal](/features/consumer-portal).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send messages">
    When something happens in your app, [send a message](/sending/messages) with an event type and a JSON payload. Hookline fans it out to every matching endpoint.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hookline handles delivery">
    Every request is [signed](/security/verifying-signatures), [retried](/delivery/retry-schedule) on failure, and recorded so you and your customers can [replay](/delivery/replaying-messages) it later.
  </Step>
</Steps>
