Team members are people who can sign in to your workspace dashboard. Each member has exactly one role. Roles apply to both the test and live environments.

## Roles at a glance

| Role      | Typical use                                                     |
| --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Owner     | Billing, workspace deletion, and everything an Admin can do.    |
| Admin     | Manage members, API keys, SSO, and all settings.                |
| Developer | Manage applications, endpoints, event types, and replay messages. |
| Viewer    | Read-only access to the dashboard and logs.                     |

For the full permission matrix, see [Roles and permissions](/security/roles-and-permissions).

## Invite a member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the members page">
    Go to **Settings** > **Members** and click **Invite member**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter details">
    Enter one or more email addresses, separated by commas. Choose a role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    Click **Send invite**. Each person receives an email with a link that stays valid for 7 days.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Owners and Admins can invite members. Admins can't invite new Owners.

<Note>
  If your workspace uses [single sign-on](/account/sso) with SCIM, your identity provider manages members. Manual invitations are turned off.
</Note>

## Change a member's role

1. Go to **Settings** > **Members**.
2. Click the role next to the member's name.
3. Select the new role.

The change takes effect on the member's next request. A workspace must always have at least one Owner.

## Remove a member

Click the menu next to the member's name and select **Remove from workspace**. The member is signed out immediately.

Removing a member doesn't delete the API keys they created. Review **Settings** > **API keys** and roll any keys the person had access to. See [API keys](/security/api-keys).

## Pending invitations

Pending invitations appear at the bottom of the members list. From there you can:

- Click **Resend** to send the email again and reset the 7-day expiry.
- Click **Revoke** to cancel the invitation.
