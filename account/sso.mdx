Single sign-on (SSO) lets members sign in to Hookline with your company identity provider (IdP). Hookline supports SAML 2.0 and OpenID Connect (OIDC), and works with any standards-compliant IdP.

## Before you begin

- You need the **Owner** or **Admin** role.
- You need admin access to your identity provider.
- Decide which email domains belong to your company, for example `yourcompany.example`.

## Configure SSO

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="SAML 2.0">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Copy Hookline's service provider details">
        Go to **Settings** > **Single sign-on**, select **SAML 2.0**, and copy the **ACS URL** and **Entity ID**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Create an app in your IdP">
        Create a new SAML application. Paste the ACS URL and Entity ID. Set the Name ID format to **Email address**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Map attributes">
        Send `email`, `first_name`, and `last_name` attributes. Optionally send a `hookline_role` attribute with `admin`, `developer`, or `viewer`.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Upload the IdP metadata">
        Download the metadata XML from your IdP and upload it in Hookline. Click **Save**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="OIDC">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Register Hookline in your IdP">
        Create an OIDC web application. Use the **Redirect URI** shown in **Settings** > **Single sign-on** > **OIDC**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter client credentials">
        Paste the **Issuer URL**, **Client ID**, and **Client secret** from your IdP into Hookline.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Save">
        Click **Save**. Hookline requests the `openid`, `email`, and `profile` scopes.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Test and enforce SSO

1. Click **Test connection** and sign in through your IdP in the new window.
2. Verify your email domains under **Domains**. Hookline gives you a DNS TXT record to add.
3. Turn on **Require SSO** to block password sign-in for everyone with a verified domain.

<Warning>
  Keep at least one Owner who can sign in if your IdP has an outage. Owners can always use the **Emergency access** link sent to their email, which is recorded in the [audit log](/features/audit-log).
</Warning>

## Provision members with SCIM

SCIM 2.0 keeps Hookline members in sync with your IdP. When you unassign someone in the IdP, Hookline removes them from the workspace automatically.

1. Go to **Settings** > **Single sign-on** > **SCIM** and click **Generate token**.
2. In your IdP, enter the SCIM base URL `https://api.hookline.example/scim/v2` and the token.
3. Assign users or groups to the Hookline app.

Group names that match `hookline-admin`, `hookline-developer`, or `hookline-viewer` set the member's role.
