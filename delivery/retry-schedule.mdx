Endpoints go down. Deploys restart servers, certificates expire, and databases fill up. Hookline retries failed deliveries automatically, so a short outage on your customer's side doesn't lose events.

## What counts as a failure

Hookline treats an attempt as successful only when the endpoint returns a `2xx` status code within 15 seconds. Every other outcome is a failure and schedules a retry:

- A `4xx` or `5xx` status code
- A `3xx` redirect, because Hookline doesn't follow redirects
- No response within 15 seconds
- A connection error, DNS failure, or TLS error

The one exception is `410 Gone`, which disables the endpoint immediately instead of retrying. See [Endpoint health](/delivery/endpoint-health).

Retries are independent per endpoint. If a message fans out to three endpoints and one of them is down, only that endpoint's delivery is retried.

## When all attempts fail

After the eighth attempt fails, Hookline:

1. Marks the message as `failed` for that endpoint.
2. Sends a `message.attempt.exhausted` [operational webhook](/features/operational-webhooks) to you.
3. Keeps the message and its attempts in the logs for your plan's retention period.

You can then [replay the message](/delivery/replaying-messages) once the endpoint is fixed.

<Tip>
  Hookline sends a `message.attempt.failing` operational webhook after the fourth failed attempt, about 35 minutes in. Use it to alert your customer before they lose a full day of events.
</Tip>

Every attempt is recorded with its status, response code, the first 4 KB of the response body, and duration.

## Guidance for receivers

- Return `2xx` only after you've safely stored the event. If you return `2xx` and then crash, Hookline won't retry.
- Return `5xx` or `429` for temporary problems you want Hookline to retry.
- Expect duplicates and [deduplicate by `webhook-id`](/security/replay-protection#deduplicate-by-message-id).
